Put your handwriting photos here (they are git-ignored so they don't end up on GitHub).

Tips for a good style sample:
- 2-5 lines of normal writing, ~4-8 words per line, in the pen you want to imitate.
- Include a mix of letters, a capital, and a digit or two.
- Shoot from directly above in even light; fill the frame with the writing.
- Write a matching `.txt` with one line of text per handwritten line.
